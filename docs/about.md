# About Me

I am Iqrar Rizvi, a QA analyst who also trains people and builds small product and design pieces.

I started in customer support, moved into training new hires and writing the help articles they used, and then into quality assurance, where my team and I automated our regression tests. Each step taught me the same lesson: people do their best work when someone has turned how a thing works into something they can use on their first day.

That is what I like building most: a lesson plan, a clear page, a test that tells you exactly what broke, a decision written down with the reasons behind it.

## What I Do

- **Product owner and product manager work:** Sorting a long list of ideas into what to build first, writing down why, and measuring whether it worked.
- **Front end and design work:** Making things you can see and click, and explaining the choices behind them. Those pieces are planned and come next.
- **Quality and risk:** Deciding what to check when time is short, and telling the people who decide what was not covered.
- **Training:** Courses, lesson plans and quizzes, in my [training portfolio](https://rizvihiqrar.github.io/training-portfolio/).

## How I Work

I work in small pieces and show my reasoning, including what I chose not to do. Each case study uses real numbers from my own projects, labels a rule of thumb as one, and ends with what I would change.
